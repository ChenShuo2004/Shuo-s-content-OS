# Rubric v0

Each dimension is scored 1-5.

| Dimension | Score in this fake channel |
|---|---|
| Hook | Does the opening name a clear creator pain? |
| Clarity | Can a reader retell the idea in one sentence? |
| Specificity | Does it show a concrete workflow or artifact? |
| Resonance | Does it reduce anxiety or sharpen ambition? |
| Distribution | Would another builder quote or forward it? |

Composite is the sum of all dimensions.
