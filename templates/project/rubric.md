# Rubric v0

This is a generic starter rubric. Replace it slowly with your own evidence-backed rules.

Each dimension is scored 1-5.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook | Slow, unclear opening | Problem appears but late | Immediate tension or question |
| Clarity | Reader cannot summarize it | Main point is visible | One clean idea, easy to retell |
| Specificity | Abstract claims only | Some examples | Concrete scenes, stakes, names, constraints |
| Resonance | Low emotional charge | Recognizable pain or desire | Strong identity, emotion, or contradiction |
| Distribution | Hard to quote or share | Some share potential | Easy to forward, quote, argue with, or reuse |

## Composite

Start with equal weights:

```text
composite = Hook + Clarity + Specificity + Resonance + Distribution
```

Do not tune weights until you have at least 5 retros.
